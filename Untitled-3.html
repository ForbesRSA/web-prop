Our Story

Eric Kayz Barbershop was started with one simple idea — every client should walk out feeling like a celebrity. Based in Bezuidenhout Valley, Johannesburg, what began as a small local barbershop has grown into a trusted spot for fresh cuts and warm hospitality.

How It Started

Eric had a passion for cutting hair and a dream of building a barbershop where people didn't just get a haircut, but an experience. From day one, the focus was on hygiene, skill, and making every customer feel welcome the moment they walked through the door.

What We Believe In

We believe a great haircut is about more than style — it's about confidence. Every client who sits in our chair leaves looking sharp and feeling like the best version of themselves.

Today

Today, Eric Kayz Barbershop continues to serve the Johannesburg community with the same care and attention to detail it started with — one fresh cut at a time.
